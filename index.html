Position the face

Drag the photo and use the slider until the face fills the circle. That circle is exactly what ends up on the character.

Match setup

Player 1 uses a gamepad or the keyboard. Plug in a second gamepad and Player 2 becomes human automatically.

Gamepad — Left stick move · Up on the stick or d-pad jumps · Cross jump · Square attack · Circle special · Triangle grab · R1 shield · Options menu
Keyboard (solo) — A/D move, W jump, S fast-fall · J attack · K special · U grab · I smash · L shield · arrows are the c-stick · Esc menu

Two players, one keyboard — set one slot to Keys: WASD and the other to Keys: arrows.
WASD side: A/D move, W jump, S down · F attack · G special · T grab · R smash · H shield
Arrows side: arrows move, up jumps, down ducks · . attack · / special · ; grab · , smash · ' shield

Up only jumps on its own — hold it with attack, smash or special and you get the up version of that move instead.

Face photos: tap a name above, pick a photo, then drag and zoom until the face fills the circle. Screenshots work fine — just zoom in on the face.

Attacks change with direction. Attack with no direction jabs; hold left/right, up, or down for a different move. Same in the air — five distinct aerials.

Three ways to smash: flick the stick and press attack (hold attack to charge) · push the right stick in any direction (arrow keys on keyboard) · or hold L1 / I and a direction.

Specials: Circle on its own, or with left/right, up, or down for four different moves per fighter. Up-special only works once per trip through the air.

Items: stand over one and press grab or attack to pick it up. Then attack uses it, grab throws it, and the right stick throws it in that direction or swings it hard. Press grab as something flies at you to catch it. The bat swings three times before it breaks.

Dodging: shield in mid-air is an air dodge, once per trip through the air. Rolls and spot dodges have invincibility frames.

Ledges: falling past the platform edge grabs it automatically. From the hang, press toward the stage to climb, jump to hop up, attack for a get-up attack, or away/down to drop.

Two players: pair both controllers to the phone, then set Player 1 and Player 2 above. Controller order is the order they were paired.

Soft platforms: jump up through them, and press down + jump to drop back off.

Shield + left/right rolls. Shield + down spot-dodges. Grab beats shield. Tap a CPU fighter on the stage to change its difficulty.
